# Presentation Studio — quick start for partners

**Open `MHWG_Presentation_Studio.html` in Edge.** That one file is the whole program. It never
connects to the internet; client information stays on your computer.

## The short version

1. **Start** — pick what you are making, drop in the reports, add your notes.
2. **Edit** — the draft is already laid out. Fill in the yellow `[[blanks]]`.
3. **Copilot** — *optional*: copy one prompt, paste it into Copilot, paste the answer back.
   It writes the words around the figures.
4. **Finish** — check the list, Export PDF.

A portfolio review from a Croesus report takes a couple of minutes.

---

## 1 · Start

**Pick what you are making.**

| | What you drop in | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Portfolio review** | Croesus portfolio report (PDF) | At a glance · your accounts · how it's invested · performance · recommendations · next steps |
| **Financial plan summary** | The financial plan (PDF) | Where you stand · goals and how well they're funded · closing the gap · projection · education · protection · estate · assumptions · next steps |
| **Annual review** | Both | The two together, with an agenda and one set of recommendations |
| **Investment recommendation** | Croesus report (optional) | Our recommendations (household summary) · one page per account, with the model portfolio |
| **Prospect meeting** | Your notes (dictate them) | Thank you · what we heard · your situation today · what matters most · what we would recommend · how we would invest · planning beyond the investments · how we work · what happens next · what to bring |
| Proposal, topic write-up, blank | — | House templates to fill in |

**Planning topics, ready written.** On Edit, the **+ Add** list has sections you can drop into any
document: RRSP to RRIF, CPP and OAS timing, pension options, retirement income, TFSA or RRSP,
spousal RRSP, tax-loss selling, debt or investing, corporate investing, RESP, FHSA, estate,
insurance, market update and fees. Each is written in plain language with yellow blanks for what
is specific to the client, and Copilot fills those from your notes.

**Drop in your reports.** Drag the PDFs onto the box, or click *choose files*. The report is
read **on this computer** and the draft lays itself out: every figure comes straight from the
report. You'll see what was found ("5 accounts, 28 holdings, returns for 7 periods…").

- Croesus account numbers tell it the account type: **A/B** non-registered (CAD/USD, or
  corporate), **S** RRSP / LIRA / spousal RRSP, **J** TFSA, **V** RESP. Accounts are named the
  house way: *Robert's RRSP*, *Joint Non-Registered*, *Family RESP*.
- A report it doesn't recognize? It offers to have Copilot read it (copy a prompt, attach the
  PDF in Copilot, paste the answer back).
- Screenshots and pictures go onto the page. Word files become sections.

**Your notes**: type or dictate (**Windows + H**) anything: what you discussed, what changed,
what you want to recommend. Copilot uses them when it writes.

**Investment recommendation only**: choose the household's **investor profile**. Each
account starts on the matching model (e.g. Balanced Growth), and the summary page shows
whether the combined mix sits inside the profile's equity range.

## 2 · Edit

- **Click any text on the page and type over it.** Press Enter (or click away) to finish.
- **Yellow `[[blanks]]`** are the only things you must supply. Press **Next blank** above the
  pages: it selects the next one so you just type over it. Finish counts them too.
- **"What we recommend"** starts with suggestions drawn from the data: a TFSA for whoever
  doesn't have one, a large single position, legacy holdings with no value, RESP grants…
  Delete the ones that don't apply.
- Point at a block for its toolbar: **+ Add below · ✦ Copilot · move · duplicate · delete**.
  Click it to see all of its settings on the right.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes anything.

## 3 · Copilot (optional)

1. Press **Copy the prompt**. It contains your notes, the figures from the reports, and every
   pre-written box.
2. In Copilot: paste it and press Enter. (You can attach the reports too.)
3. Copy Copilot's whole answer and paste it into box 3. It goes in by itself.

Read it over: Copilot rewrites the words, but the figures stay exactly as the report has
them. To redo just one paragraph, point at it on the page and press **✦ Copilot**.

## 4 · Finish

- The checklist shows yellow blanks left, wording to read over, anything that would be cut
  off, and missing cover details. Click an item to go to it.
- Leave **DRAFT** on until compliance has reviewed it.
- **Export PDF** → in the print window: *Save as PDF*, margins *None*, **Background graphics on**.
- **Save** writes a `.mhwg.json` working file to Downloads. **Open** it next time to keep working.

*Look & pages* (cover style, accent, contents page…) and *Team, contact & disclosures* are at
the bottom of Finish, already set the house way.
